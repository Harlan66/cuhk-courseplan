Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.          Major Programme:

Upon programme completion, graduates will have acquired the ability to:

(1)      demonstrate mastery of fundamental concepts involved in meeting the health and social care needs of the aging population, including

- the biological, physical, psychological, and social aspects of ageing;

- successful aging;

- chronic disease management for older people;

- community/residential care service planning;

- interdisciplinary aged care service planning, administration, coordination, and delivery; (K)

(2)      demonstrate the skills needed in assessing and delivering evidence-based care for older people in the community, rehabilitative, and residential care settings; (K/S)

(3)      plan and coordinate aged care services within and across these settings; (K/S)

(4)      critically evaluate the impacts of the changing health care, social, and cultural contexts and contemporary policies on decision-making for aged care service planning, coordination, evaluation, and re-engineering; (K/V)

(5)      assess, interpret, and apply innovative health and social care models in evaluating and re-engineering aged care services within and across the community, rehabilitative and residential care settings; (K/S/V)

(6)      work effectively with other professionals involved in aged care service provision and apply the interdisciplinary collaborative care model in planning and coordinating these services; (K/S/V)

(7)       demonstrate effective communication and interpersonal skills in dealing with older people, their caregivers and families, and other professionals involved in the provision of aged care services; (K/S/V)

(8)       demonstrate the ability to advocate best practices and assume a leadership role in coordinating and fostering interprofessional practice; (K/S/V)

(9)       drive social innovation in practice and lead innovation in aged care; (K/S/V)

(10)  exhibit professionalism in practice and adhere to legal and ethical standards of aged care; (K/S)

(11)  demonstrate mastery of research skills and utilise research evidence in the provision of aged care services; (K/S) and

(12)  display personal and social responsibility and engage in lifelong learning and personal development. (V)

 

K = Knowledge outcomes       S = Skills outcomes       V = Values and attitudes outcomes
