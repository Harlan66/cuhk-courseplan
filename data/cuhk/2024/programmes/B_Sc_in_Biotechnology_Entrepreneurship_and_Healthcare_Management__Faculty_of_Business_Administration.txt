Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.     Major Programme:




The programme aims to equip students with entrepreneurial mindset for producing ground-breaking ideas, integrated knowledge in life/health sciences, data analytics and business management to provide innovative/interdisciplinary solutions, and also ability to communicate with collaborators of diverse background and manage/advance/provide solution for healthcare operations and translate medical/scientific advances into practice.

 




Upon completion of the programme, students will have:




Knowledge outcomes




(1)

	

acquired understanding on state-of-the-art biotechnology and application perspective;




(2)

	

understood the fundamental concepts in business management with advanced knowledge in data analytics and financial management;




(3)

	

acquired the contemporary concepts of public health and the interactions between determinants of health at individual, local and global levels;




(4)

	

explained from global perspective the burden of disease, the links between health and development, and approaches to international cooperation to monitor, promote and protect health; and




(5)

	

described important issues involved in public health advocacy, including ethical reasoning, civic engagement, and social justice.




 

	

 




Skills outcomes




(1)

	

analyzed critical information related to biotechnology and make business plan;




(2)

	

used data analytics tools to make data-driven decisions and formulate strategies;




(3)

	

demonstrated leadership skills and can communicate effectively with stakeholders in both public and commercial sectors to facilitate collaborations and manage innovation processes;




(4)

	

assessed the health status, social concern, needs and equity of different populations contributing to the use of health services and health promotion practices; and




(5)

	

utilized evidence-based approach to inquire, analyze and criticize contemporary public health issues, and design framework for primary/secondary/tertiary care and prevention.




 

	

 




Values and attitude outcomes




(1)

	

had a positive attitude in applying biotechnology to benefit the human society and contribute to sustainable development;




(2)

	

applied market orientation in both public and private sectors;




(3)

	

demonstrated appreciation of the importance of primary care in population health;




(4)

	

acknowledged the cross-cultural impacts on actual and perceived health needs; and




(5)

	

upheld core value of social equity and justice.
