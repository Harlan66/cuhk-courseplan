Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.

	

Major Programme:




Knowledge and comprehension




Students will have acquired the following knowledge:




(1)

	

mathematical analysis,




(2)

	

linear algebra,




(3)

	

computer programming for mathematics,




(4)

	

further knowledge in at least one direction:




-

	

(Enrichment Stream) Knowledge in mathematics enabling the students to have solid foundation for further studies at the graduate school level; or




-

	

(Other streams) Knowledge in a specific field together with the mathematics applicable to that field, for example, Computational and Applied Mathematics Stream, Computational Big Data Analytics Stream.




(5)

	

mathematical literacy sufficient for various professions, for example, research, education, financial technology, information technology, data science, artificial intelligence, etc.




 

	

 




Skills




Generic Competencies




Students will have acquired a set of generic competencies.




(1)

	

ability to understand broad mathematical literature at the university level independently;




(2)

	

awareness of mathematical problems arising in generic situations;




(3)

	

ability to formulate such problems mathematically, to find ways to solve them;




(4)

	

ability to communicate progress of mathematical problem solving effectively;




(5)

	

computer literacy and knowledge to use mathematical software in solving problems.




 

	

 




Sectors of Professional Skills

(1)

(2)

(3)

Financial sector




(5)

Upon graduation, our students will be able to apply their mathematical knowledge generically in their future profession in a way that is unique for mathematically trained experts.  They should be able to




(1)

	

appreciate and advocate ways of thinking with logical rigour,




(2)

	

appreciate and take advantage of quantitative and analytical exactitude,




(3)

	

appreciate the contribution of mathematics to the improvement and formation of modern society built upon quantitative natural sciences.




 

	

 




2.

	

Minor Programme:




The minor programme of study aims at providing students with good mathematical knowledge to support their professional development in today’s knowledge society. In brief, upon completion of the programme, we anticipate our students to have acquired the capability of formulating and modeling problems in a logical sound and quantitatively efficient way, which is typical in mathematical sciences.




 

	

 




Knowledge and comprehension




Students will have acquired the following knowledge:




(1)

	

Calculus or linear algebra,




(2)

	

mathematical literacy sufficient for supporting a particular profession, including but not limited to, research, education, financial technology, information technology, data science, artificial intelligence, etc.




(3)

	

model formulation related to a particular profession.




 

	

 




Skills




Students will have acquired a set of generic competencies listed below.




(1)

	

ability to understand broad mathematical literature related to their own professional development at the university level independently;




(2)

	

awareness of mathematical problems arising in generic situations;




(3)

	

ability to formulate such problems mathematically, to find ways to model them;




(4)

	

ability to communicate progress of mathematical modelling effectively.
