Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.          Major Programme:

Expected learning outcomes:

(1)   Knowledge Outcome (K): Understanding of fundamental concepts and intellectual perspectives in political science and public administration, and the ability to participate and contribute to current debates in public policies and problems, with both local and global perspectives.

 

(2)   Skills Outcome (S): Mastery of essential analytical skills, particularly the abilities to engage in independent and critical thinking, to make coherent and consistent arguments, and to critically evaluate alternative arguments in a constructive fashion; mastery of bilingual communication skills; competency in qualitative and quantitative political science methodologies as well as in tackling practical problems; ability to work as a team leader and a team member; and aptitude to continue with life-long learning.

 

(3)   Value Outcome (V): Awareness of the importance of politics in daily life and studies; appreciation and tolerance of different perspectives in understanding politics and interpreting political phenomena; and a sense of purpose, civic responsibility, global citizenship, and commitment in life.

 

2.          Minor Programme:

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
