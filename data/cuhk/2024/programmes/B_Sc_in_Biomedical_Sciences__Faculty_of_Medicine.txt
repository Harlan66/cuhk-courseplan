Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.      Major Programme




Upon completion of the Programme, the graduates will have:




1. demonstrated good knowledge and understanding of




·         anatomy, biochemistry, pharmacology, physiology, molecular biology, medical genetics, etc. and selected advanced areas of biomedical sciences (i.e. specialized topics);

·         human diseases – pathophysiology, pathology, diagnosis and genetic counseling;

·         biomedical laboratory techniques and related safety procedures;

·         biostatistics and research methods;

·         ethical and legal issues in biomedical science;

·         healthcare system and pharmaceutical management;

·         legal perspectives on intellectual property. 




 

	

 




2. acquired practical skills to




·         perform a wide range of biomedical laboratory techniques competently;

·         know how to make use of a range of modern information technologies;

·         organize data and ideas for effective presentations using a variety of formats (e.g. oral, poster);

·         work effectively and cooperatively within a group.




 

	

 




3. acquired cognitive/thinking skills to




·         interpret and critically evaluate laboratory data;

·         critically review scientific literature;

·         develop own ideas through critical appraisal and integration of known concepts, principles and published literature;

·         design a research project, collect, analyze, interpret and evaluate research data; recognize, analyze and respond to the moral, ethical and social issues related to advances in biomedical science knowledge and research;

·         recognize advances and future development in biomedical sciences from local, regional and global perspectives.




 




4. acquired skills to




·         formulate a career development plan;

·         demonstrate an autonomous and reflective approach to experiential and lifelong learning;

·         demonstrate literacy, numeracy and problem-solving skills;

·         apply knowledge and skills to career development in education, management, laboratory-based services, postgraduate studies or research in biomedical sciences and related clinical or healthcare-related disciplines.
