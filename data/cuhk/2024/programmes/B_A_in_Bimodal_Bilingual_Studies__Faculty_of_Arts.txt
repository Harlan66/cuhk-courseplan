Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.          Major Programme:

Upon completing the programme, students will have:

1.      Achieved a new understanding of how knowledge of language is organized and transmitted through the visual-spatial modality, as opposed to spoken languages, organized and transmitted in fundamentally different ways;

2.      Appreciated how this new understanding can potentially transform the society’s perception about sign language, giving it a linguistic status that is equal to spoken languages;

3.      To be able to evaluate critically the development of bimodal bilingualism in society from the perspective of information accessibility and effective communication in domains of education, social and public communication, child care, counselling and clinical services; and

4.      To be proficient in spoken languages and sign languages in order to undertake responsibilities in professional settings which call for such linguistic skills.




 




2.             Minor Programme:

Upon completion of the programme, the student will have:

1.        a reasonable level of communicative competence in HKSL, including being able to comprehend and interact with deaf people in HKSL with a reasonable level of confidence;

2.        an understanding of the visual communication strategies of Deaf people;

3.        knowledge of Deaf culture and the readiness for appreciating linguistic and cultural diversity in society;

4.        an understanding that deafness is not a disability and the Deaf Community can be viewed as a linguistic minority group with its unique culture;

5.        a general understanding of the grammar of HKSL especially how it differs from the local spoken languages; and

6.        knowledge of the potential adoption of HKSL in social, education and professional practices.
