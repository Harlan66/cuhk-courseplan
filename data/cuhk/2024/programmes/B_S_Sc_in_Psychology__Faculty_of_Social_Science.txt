Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.

	

Major Programme:




 

	

 




Graduate from this programme will have acquired knowledge, Skills, and Values Consistent With the Scientific and Applied Aspects of Psychology




(1)

	

Knowledge base of psychology




(2)

	

Research methods in psychology




(3)

	

Critical thinking skills in psychology




(4)

	

Application of psychology




(5)

	

Values in psychology




Graduate from this programme will have acquired knowledge, Skills, and Values Consistent With Personal Growth




(1)

	

Information and technological literacy




(2)

	

Communication skills




(3)

	

Sociocultural and international awareness




(4)

	

Commitment to lifelong learning, personal and career development




 

	

 




2.

	

Psychology and Linguistics Double Major Programme:




 

	

 




Upon completing the programme, students will:




(1)

	

be able to describe a wide range of language and psychological concepts and theories. (K)




(2)

	

have developed ideas across the areas of linguistics, psychology and communication. (K)




(3)

	

have profound understanding of the cognitive processes involved in language comprehension, production, as well as language disorders. (K)




(4)

	

have developed effective reading strategies and writing ability for carrying out language-related research. (K)




(5)

	

have acquired critical thinking and analytical skills in analysing and evaluating scientific research evidence. (S)




(6)

	

have obtained a sharpened awareness of the languages used in the local community through their abilities in analysing languages and discourses. (V)




(7)

	

have new perspectives on written and spoken language, communication, and cognitive science. (K&S)




(8)

	

have understood the consequences of learning disorders of spoken and written languages. (V)




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes     S = Skills outcomes     V = Values and attitude outcomes




 

	

 




3.

	

Psychology & Journalism and Communication Double Major Programme:




 

	

 




Upon completion of the Double Major Programme, students will have acquired:




Knowledge outcomes:




(1)

	

foundational knowledge in both psychology and communication studies;




(2)

	

specialized knowledge of the communication and psychological principles behind user-machine interactions;




(3)

	

basic understanding of computer coding




Skills outcomes:




(1)

	

basic skills in media, technology and interface design, including computer coding and use of specific programming language(s);




(2)

	

the ability to conduct experiments for examining how users interact with media and technologies;




(3)

	

the ability to conduct research to evaluate user and market responses to media technologies




Values and attitude outcomes:




(1)

	

an appreciation of how technologies can improve social life via appropriate design from a human-centered perspective;




(2)

	

an understanding of the possible ethical issues involved in media and technology design and development.




 

	

 




4.

	

Minor Programme:




 

	

 




Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
