Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.      Major Programme

At the end of the programme, students should be able to:

 

K

	

 Knowledge outcomes




K.1

	

Describe the fundamental concepts in community health




K.2

	

Explain the theoretical models and frameworks related to health promotion




K.3

	

Describe the roles of individuals and families with self-care and self-management skills, and the community in diseases prevention and health promotion




 

	

 




S

	

Skill outcomes




S.1

	

Assess individual and community needs for health promotion




S.2

	

Plan effective health promotion programme




S.3

	

Implement health promotion plan




S.4

	

Conduct evaluation and research related to health promotion




S.5

	

Administer and manage health promotion programme




S.6

	

Serve as a health promotion resource person




 

	

 




V

	

Value outcomes




V.1

	

Demonstrate respect for cultural diversity and sensitivity




V.2

	

Demonstrate dedication to sustainable development




V.3

	

Demonstrate care for social equity and justice
