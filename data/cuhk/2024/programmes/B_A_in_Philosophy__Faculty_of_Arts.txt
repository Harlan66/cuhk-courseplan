Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.

	

Major Programme:




Graduates from the programme will have:




(1)

	

acquired a sound knowledge of the Eastern and Western (both continental and analytic) philosophical traditions (K/S);




(2)

	

gained an understanding of the historical and cultural backgrounds of philosophical thoughts (K/S);




(3)

	

gained the ability to read and interpret classical philosophical texts, to analyze the content, and to evaluate the arguments (K/S);




(4)

	

acquired a grasp of the central issues of philosophy and to reflect on their contemporary relevance (K/S/V);




(5)

	

learnt basic concepts and methods in logic, to assess arguments in various contexts, and to identify and analyze informal fallacies (K/S);




(6)

	

gained the ability to engage in critical discussion, to put forward and defend their own ideas, and to master the expository and critical skills for argumentative writing (K/S);




(7)

	

fostered a sensibility toward ethical, social, political, and cultural issues (K/S/V);




(8)

	

appreciated the role and importance of philosophical reflection in various matters and to bring it to bear on things that matter in life (K/S/V);




(9)

	

become an independent and critical thinker and to be equipped with life-long learning capacities (K/S/V).




K = Knowledge outcomes; S = Skills outcomes; V = Value and attitude outcomes

 




2.

	

Minor Programme:




Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
