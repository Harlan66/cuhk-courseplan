Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.

	

Major Programme:




 




Through the course of their studies, graduates will have learnt:




(1)

	

to read, understand, and appreciate literatures in English, specifically in the major genres of poetry, fiction, and drama; (K/S/V)




(2)

	

to understand and apply the systematic study of language; (K/S)




(3)

	

to develop an understanding and appreciation of the local and global dimensions of English, including multilingualism and multiculturalism; (K/V)




(4)

	

to produce creative and scholarly work in high quality written English, using a variety of styles, both formal and informal, academic and nonacademic; (K/S)




(5)

	

to communicate effectively in spoken English in a manner appropriate to the communicative and cultural context, including making effective oral presentations using appropriate technology; and (S/V)




(6)

	

to continue building upon their knowledge and skills after graduation, with an interest in learning as a lifelong process. (K/S/V)




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes     S = Skills outcomes     V = Values & attitude outcomes




 

	

 




2.

	

English and History Double Major Programme:




 

	

 




Upon completing the Programme, students will have acquired:




(1)

	

command of appropriate academic and creative English language skills; (K)




(2)

	

progress in humanities research, particularly linguistic and historical research; (K)




(3)

	

deepened understanding of both English and Chinese culture and history; (K)




(4)

	

critical and creative thinking; (S)




(5)

	

communication skills and computer literacy; (S)




(6)

	

self-managed learning, adaptability and problem solving skills; (S)




(7)

	

ability to appreciate global cultural diversity, exchanges and mutual dependence; (V)




(8)

	

ability to value and appreciate the beauty of language and culture and the pleasure of humanities research; and (V)




(9)

	

ability to consolidate cosmopolitan identity and cultural openness of Hong Kong. (V)




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes     S = Skills outcomes     V = Values & attitude outcomes




 

	

 




3.

	

Minor Programme:




 

	

 




(i)

	

English




Upon completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.




 

	

 




(ii)

	

Creative Writing in English




Upon completion of the Minor Programme, students will have acquired the following outcomes:




(a)

	

Community engagement – students and graduates equipped with creative writing skills will be able to contribute meaningfully to the cultural life of Hong Kong. They will be able to organize and participate in English language literary readings and creative writing workshops and enhance the cultural life of Hong Kong;




(b)

	

Educational needs – graduates of the course who become teachers will be equipped to teach creative writing to their students and assess creative writing in English in schools; and




(c)

	

Intrapersonal needs – as creative writing deals with personal feelings that may not be easily expressed in analytical academic writings, it helps students in their search for meaning in their lives.
