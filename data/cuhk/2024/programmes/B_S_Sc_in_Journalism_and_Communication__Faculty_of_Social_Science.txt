Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.

	

Major Programme:




 

	

 




Knowledge outcomes




(1)

	

A solid theoretical and practical basis for studying and analyzing communication phenomena.




(2)

	

Knowledge of theoretical and practical skills and the ability to apply those skills to convey informational, creative and persuasive messages and address social problems for the common good in local and international contexts.




(3)

	

Specialized spectrum of knowledge characteristic of the professional areas of advertising and public relations, broadcasting, creative and new media, journalism, or communication studies.




 

Skill outcomes




Generic skill outcomes




(1)

	

Analytical skills




(2)

	

Problem solving skills




(3)

	

Critical thinking




(4)

	

Interpersonal, social and leadership skills




Specific skill outcomes




(1)

	

Multimedia production and presentation skills




(2)

	

Professional and creative writing skills




(3)

	

Information literacy




 

Value outcomes




(1)

	

Professional ethics conducive to communicating messages in a truthful, accurate, and fair manner.




(2)

	

Sociocultural awareness of and concern for contemporary social and media issues.




(3)

	

Commitment and passion towards professional development, intellectual curiosity and life-long learning.




 

	

 




2.

	

Journalism and Communication & Psychology Double Major Programme:




 

	

 




Upon completion of the Double Major Programme, students will have acquired:




 

Knowledge outcomes:




(1)

	

foundational knowledge in both psychology and communication studies;




(2)

	

specialized knowledge of the communication and psychological principles behind user-machine interactions;




(3)

	

basic understanding of computer coding.




 

Skills outcomes:




(1)

	

basic skills in media, technology and interface design, including computer coding and use of specific programming language(s);




(2)

	

the ability to conduct experiments for examining how users interact with media and technologies;




(3)

	

the ability to conduct research to evaluate user and market responses to media technologies.




 

Values and attitude outcomes:




(1)

	

an appreciation of how technologies can improve social life via appropriate design from a human-centered perspective;




(2)

	

an understanding of the possible ethical issues involved in media and technology design and development.




 

	

 




3.

	

Journalism and Communication & Sociology Double Major Programme:




 

	

 




Upon completion of the Double Major Programme, students will have:




 

K = Knowledge outcomes




(1)

	

gained a breath of knowledge in Sociology, and Journalism and Communication




(2)

	

developed a high level of critical thinking and analytical reasoning skills through cross-disciplinary training




(3)

	

expanded and extended intellectual flexibility and diversity




(4)

	

developed knowledge essential to fostering global and regional sustainable development




(5)

	

harnessed the synergies between two influential social science disciplines




 

S = Skills outcomes




(1)

	

developed multiple skills in addressing complex world problems




(2)

	

acquired the ability to theorize, design and execute social research critically and independently




(3)

	

acquired the ability to formulate evidence-based responses for policy or professional communication




(4)

	

mastered informative and persuasive communication and writing skills




(5)

	

acquired analytical and innovative problem-solving abilities in preparation for Industry 4.0




 

V = Values and attitude outcomes




(1)

	

worked under the mentoring of senior academics and qualified practitioners




(2)

	

developed a strong sense of social consciousness, freedom of expression, and social responsibility towards achieving sustainable development




(3)

	

acquired the ability to instill a strong commitment to professional ethics in communication professions and in conducting social research




(4)

	

developed autonomy, responsibility and accountability in individual or collaborative work




 

	

 




4.

	

Minor Programme:




 

	

 




Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
