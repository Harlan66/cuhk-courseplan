Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.

	

Major Programme:




Graduates of the B.Sc. Programme in Exercise Science and Health Education (ESHE) will be able to:




(1)

	

demonstrate specialist knowledge and professional skills in Exercise Science and Health Education; (K/S)




(2)

	

plan and implement a programme of investigative work; (K/S/V)




(3)

	

demonstrate an informed, critical, and innovative approach to learning through understanding and applying knowledge and skills learned from the field of exercise science towards the advancement of human health, performance and quality of life, through effective education, promotion, evaluation, research and development; (K/S/V)




(4)

	

synthesize and evaluate strategies to enhance professionalism in administrative and managerial roles in sports, exercise science and health sectors; (K/S/V)




(5)

	

gather and use information as a strategic resource to contribute creatively to the development of Exercise Science and Health Education; (K/S/V)




(6)

	

display a critical and creative attitude in their professional life; (K/S/V) and




(7)

	

apply their knowledge and skills in exercise science and health practices. (K/S)




 

K = Knowledge outcomes     S = Skills outcomes       V = Values and attitude outcomes
