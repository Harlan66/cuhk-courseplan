Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.          Major Programme:

The Social Work Programme prepares students to be professionally competent social workers. At the end of the course of studies, students will have:

(1)   possessed basic knowledge and understanding of the values of Social Work (K/V);

(2)   identified with the humanistic values and had a genuine concern for people especially for the vulnerable groups (V);

(3)   had commitment to the well-being of clients, and to social justice (V);

(4)   understood the social, cultural and psychological factors contributing to the needs and problems of people (K/S);

(5)   been equipped with knowledge and skills on social work research, administration and policy (K/S); and

(6)   been equipped with knowledge and skills in working with individual, group and community/society (K/S).

V = Value       K = Knowledge      S = Skill

 

2.          Minor Programmes:

(i)     Minor in Social Welfare

(ii)    Minor in Social Policy

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
