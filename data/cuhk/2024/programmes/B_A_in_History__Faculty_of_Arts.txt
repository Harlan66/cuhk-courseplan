Explanatory Notes:

	

 




1.

	

HIST courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.




2.

	

Subject to approval by the Major Programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the 1st Major Programme only. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have opted out are required to complete and fulfill all graduation requirements by following the study scheme of the 1st Major Programme as a single major. Students should be aware that such decision may result in a delay in graduation because the requirements of the single major study scheme differ from those of the Double Major Programme.




[a]

	

Students who have prior knowledge of Japanese before admission may be exempted from taking JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and 2480. They should seek the advice of Department of Japanese Studies to take substitute courses in accordance with their academic qualifications as follows:




 

	

[i]   

	

Students who passed placement test and exempted from taking any or all required language course(s) (JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and/or 2480) are required to take corresponding units of Language courses at 3000 or 4000 level specified under 3(b) above, to substitute for the relevant course(s). These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.




 

	

[ii]

	

Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N2) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with at least 12 units of Language Courses at 3000 or 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.




 

	

[iii]

	

Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N1) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with at least 9 units of Language Courses at 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.




 

	

[iv]

	

Native Japanese students are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with or without any Language Courses, to substitute for the four relevant courses. If they choose to take Language Courses, they are allowed to take Language Courses at 4000 level only, subject to the approval of the course teacher. If they choose to take non-language courses, they should take at least 12 units of elective courses at 2000 or above level.




[b]

	

Subject to Department’s approval, native speaker of Japanese can be exempted from the fulfillment of one JASP 4000 level language course, and take 3 units of JASP course as a substitution.




[c]

	

LING1000 which counts towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 1 Faculty Package cannot be used for the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 3(a) Non-language Courses.




[d]

	

These courses are only offered to students before their resident study in Japan.

Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.

	

Major Programme:

 




At the end of the course of their studies, students will have:




(1)

	

gained a solid training of empirical methods for the study of history;




(2)

	

mastered knowledge of diversified areas of historical study (national history, comparative history and public history);




(3)

	

developed critical thinking on diversified areas of historical study;




(4)

	

produced creative work and conduct scholarly research;




(5)

	

learnt to communicate effectively in academic discourse and the professional workplace, including making effective written and oral presentations using appropriate tool and technology;




(6)

	

developed the ability to apply the knowledge and skills of historical study in different context after graduation; and




(7)

	

developed the ability and habit to continue building upon their knowledge and skills after graduation, with an interest of learning as a lifelong process.




 

	

 




2.

	

History and English Double Major Programme:




 

Upon completing the Programme, students will have acquired:




(1)

	

command of appropriate academic and creative English language skills; (K)




(2)

	

progress in humanities research, particularly linguistic and historical research; (K)




(3)

	

deepened understanding of both English and Chinese culture and history; (K)




(4)

	

critical and creative thinking; (S)




(5)

	

communication skills and computer literacy; (S)




(6)

	

self-managed learning, adaptability and problem solving skills; (S)




(7)

	

ability to appreciate global cultural diversity, exchanges and mutual dependence; (V)




(8)

	

ability to value and appreciate the beauty of language and culture and the pleasure of humanities research; and (V)




(9)

	

ability to consolidate cosmopolitan identity and cultural openness of Hong Kong. (V)




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes       S = Skills outcomes        V = Values and attitude outcomes




 

	

 




3.

	

History and Japanese Studies Double Major Programme:




 

Upon completing the Programme, students will have acquired:




(1)

	

command of Japanese language; (K)




(2)

	

progress in humanities research, particularly historical research and Japanese Studies; (K)




(3)

	

deepened understanding of both Japanese and Chinese culture and history; (K)




(4)

	

critical and creative thinking; (S)  




(5)

	

communication skills and computer literacy; (S)  




(6)

	

self-managed learning, adaptability and problem solving skills; (S)  




(7)

	

ability to appreciate East Asian cultural diversity, exchanges and mutual dependence; (V)  




(8)

	

ability to value and appreciate the beauty of language and culture and the joy of humanities research; and (V)  




(9)

	

ability to help consolidate cosmopolitan identity and cultural openness of East Asia. (V)  




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes        S = Skills outcomes        V = Values and attitude outcomes




 

	

 




4.

	

Minor Programme:

 




(i)

	

History




(ii)

	

Cultural Heritage Studies




Upon completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.




 




(iii)

	

Public History




 

	

Upon completion of this Minor Programme, students will:




 

	

•          

	

have acquired knowledge of the origin, development, and major debates in the field of public history. (K)




 

	

•          

	

have developed an understanding of the presentation skills of public history and the ability to undertake public history projects. (S)




 

	

•          

	

have developed an understanding of ethics in the practice of public history, as well as the value of history and culture in building personal philosophy of life. (V)




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes       S = Skills outcomes        V = Values and attitude outcomes
