Risk Management Science

Applicable to students admitted in 2024-25




 




 

There is one enrichment stream in the Risk Management Science Programme: Risk Analytics Stream.  Students who got a total score of 30 or above in the best five HKDSE subjects or have obtained a cumulative GPA of 3.7 or above in the first or second year of attendance may apply for specializing in the Risk Analytics Stream.

Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.

	

Major Programme:




Graduates will have acquired the following:




Knowledge:




(1)

	

Knowledge of calculating risk and risk measures from a scientific perspective and their role in finance, insurance and related fields




(2)

	

Background knowledge of accounting, economics for business studies, financial management and investment analysis




(3)

	

Knowledge of mathematical tools for statistical analysis




(4)

	

Knowledge of computer programming




 

	

 




Professional Skills:




(1)

	

Ability to apply knowledge of statistics, accounting, economics and business studies to risk management




(2)

	

Ability to articulate and convey concepts of risk and risk management to both layman and professional




(3)

	

Ability to identify appropriate risk management measures




(4)

	

Simulation skills for risk management




(5)

	

Statistical skills for data analysis




 

	

 




General Competencies:




(1)

	

Competence in Chinese and English languages




(2)

	

Competence in using information technology




(3)

	

Analytical skills




(4)

	

Self-learning skills




 

	

 




Attitude/Value:




(1)

	

Recognize the importance of risk management




(2)

	

Appropriate attitude towards uncertainty




(3)

	

High ethical standards




 

	

 




2.

	

Minor Programme:




Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to Risk Management Science, acquired knowledge of calculating risk and risk measures from a scientific perspective and their role in finance, insurance and related fields.
