Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.          Major Programme:

At the end of the course of studies, students will have acquired :

(1)   skills to design the built environment well and appropriately;

(2)   the capacity of knowledge in humanities, technologies and professional practice of architecture and construction;

(3)   innovative and critical thinking in both the use of knowledge and the application of design skills;

(4)   a commitment to social responsibility and professional ethics.
