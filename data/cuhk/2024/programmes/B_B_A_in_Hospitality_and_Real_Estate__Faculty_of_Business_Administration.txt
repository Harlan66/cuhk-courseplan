Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.          Major Programme:

Business concepts and functions:

Graduates should have mastered different business concepts and functions so that they can lead a hospitality enterprise as an integrated business firm. They should have possessed broad-based core knowledge of general management covering accounting, finance, marketing, human resources, information technology, etc. At the same time, graduates should have mastered a deep understanding of the context of managing hospitality and tourism businesses.

 

Analytical skills:

Students should have mastered analytical skills to identify a business problem, generate and compare alternatives, and come up with a prudent solution. Students will have developed managerial skills such as communication, problem solving, analytical, critical thinking and decision making, etc throughout the curriculum. In this regard, graduates should possess a core level of generic skills related to management of hospitality businesses, including: Computer literacy/IT skills, quantitative skills and research methods, and decision making skills.

 

Professional Skills:

Graduates should have a mastery of professional skills pertaining to management of business in service industry. They should be able to operate different hospitality and tourism organizations. They should be able to identify current hospitality and tourism management problems and propose appropriate solutions.

 

Attitude/Values:

Graduates should have a positive attitude and passion toward the hospitality and tourism industry. They should be nurtured servant-leadership. They should have possessed values for excellence, leadership, creativity, service and ethics in professional and personal life. They should have developed an attitude for adapting to the changing social and environment and become an effective and responsible citizen.
