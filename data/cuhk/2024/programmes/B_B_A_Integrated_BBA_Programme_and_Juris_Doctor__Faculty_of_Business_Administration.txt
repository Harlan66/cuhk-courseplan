Explanatory Notes:




1.

	

ACCT/DOTE/FINA/IBBA/MGNT/MKTG courses at 2000 and above level (excluding ACCT2111, 2121, IBBA3040, MGNT2511 and 2512) will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.




2.

	

Students claiming Entrepreneurship Concentration are not allowed to declare Minor in Entrepreneurship and Innovation.




3.

	

In addition to fulfilling the major programme requirement of the 1st degree, students are advised to take MGNT2611 as an elective course.




[a]

	

Students who have completed 18 units of LAWS courses will be exempted from 9 units of the equivalent courses in the Juris Doctor (JD) programme.




[b]

	

Students who choose one of the following concentrations: (i) Finance, (ii) Entrepreneurship, (iii) Management of International Business, (iv) Human Resource Management, (v) Marketing, or (vi) Big Data and Quantitative Marketing are required to complete a minimum of 73 units in order to fulfill the Major programme requirement.

Explanatory Notes:




1.

	

To be eligible to proceed to the fifth year for the JD degree, students must achieve a minimum CGPA (to be specified by the Faculty of Law) after completion of the fourth year. Students may choose to opt out from the double degree programme and complete a BBA degree or a BBA degree with a Minor in Law in four years, subject to the fulfillment of the requirements specified in the relevant study scheme.




[a]

	

Students who have completed LAWS1020 and 1030 in their first three years of studies in the BBA-JD programme will be exempted from LAWS6001.




[b]

	

Students may take LAWS6089 to substitute LAWS6006.




[c]

	

Students are required to take either LAWS6901 (3-unit course) or 6902 (6-unit course). The extra 3 units completed in LAWS6902 will be counted towards the total units required for graduation.




[d]

	

LAWS6012 and 6014 will no longer be pre-requisites for admission to the PCLL Programme with effect from 2021-22. Students may still take the two courses as elective courses offered under the JD Programme.




[e]

	

Students who have completed LAWS1041 and 1042 in their first three years of studies in the BBA-JD programme will be exempted from LAWS6021.




[f]

	

Students who have completed LAWS2131 and 2132 in their first three years of studies in the BBA-JD programme will be exempted from LAWS6023.




[g]

	

Students who have completed LAWS1020, 1030, 1041, 1042, 2131 and 2132 are required to complete a minimum of 63 units in order to fulfill the programme requirement.

Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.          Major Programme:

Upon completion of the BBA programme, students will have acquired:

1. effective business communication skills (S);

2. effective analytical skills which support them in making business decisions (S);

3. the ability to integrate business knowledge and solve problems in a business environment (K/S);

4. the ability to propose business strategies in a global environment (S);

5. awareness of the ethical issues in a business context (V);

6. an understanding of the legal landscape in which business is conducted (K).

 

2.          JD Programme:

Upon completion of the JD programme, students will have acquired:

1. an understanding of the law and legal system of Hong Kong, with particular emphasis on the law in action and the dynamic interplay between law and other social phenomena. (K, V)

2. an appreciation of the common law system and its values, and its interaction with the law of China, the East Asian region and the wider world. (K, V)

3. a strong sense of ethics, civic duty, social and professional responsibility. (V)

4. awareness of the strengths and weaknesses of law as a means of regulating society in the context of competing and conflicting interests. (K, V)

5. acquired and improved skills of legal analysis and reasoning; skills of legal research; skills of oral and written expression of legal ideas; and the general critical and evaluative faculties appropriate to a postgraduate degree in law. (S)

 

K = Knowledge outcomes; S = Skills outcomes; V = Values outcomes

 

Note: The expected learning outcomes for the JD programme are only available in English.
