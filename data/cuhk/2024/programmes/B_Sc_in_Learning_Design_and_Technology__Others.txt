Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 1.     Major Programme:




The programme aims to equip students with multi-disciplinary knowledge in education, technology and science, as well as essential skills and competencies for a variety of roles in the fast-changing, information- rich world.




 




Upon completion of the programme, students will have:




·          

	

mastered the latest theoretical underpinnings of learning design and the foundational know-how of the cutting-edge learning technology; (K)




·          

	

gained inspirational insights into design, implementation and evaluation of technology-supported learning;(K/S)




·          

	

demonstrated competent technological and pedagogical skills to take on challenges in a rapidly changing world; (S)




·          

	

acquired a critical and creative mind to approach real-world problems with original and innovative solutions; (K/S)




·          

	

investigated issues from multiple perspectives through engaging in transformative learning opportunities, such as self-directed/collaborative research studies, educational/technological internships, authentic community-based service learning and organizational leaderships; (K/S/V) and




·          

	

developed the essential mindset and skillset that foster technological innovation and excellence in the field of education or other related areas. (K/S/V)




 

	

 




K = Knowledge outcomes




S = Skills outcomes




V = Values and attitude outcomes
