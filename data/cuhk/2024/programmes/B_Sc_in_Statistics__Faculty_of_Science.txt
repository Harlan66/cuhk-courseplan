Statistics

Applicable to students admitted in 2024-25




 

 




There are three streams of specialization: the stream of Data Science and Business Statistics, the stream of Statistical Learning and Data Mining and the stream of Data Analytics.  A student’s selected stream will be printed on his/her transcript.  All students are under the stream of Data Science and Business Statistics unless they apply at their final year of attendance to specialize in the stream of Statistical Learning and Data Mining or the stream of Data Analytics and select the relevant courses as prescribed below.

Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.     Major Programme:

Knowledge Outcomes

Content

Graduates will have possessed fundamental knowledge of statistics, including the following:

(1)   Basic concepts in probability and their applications

(2)   Basic concepts in statistics and their uses

(3)   Mathematical tools for statistics

(4)   Applied statistical methodologies

(5)   Applications of statistical packages in data management and analysis

 

Critical Thinking/Statistical Reasoning

Graduate will have well-developed sense of statistical consciousness, and ability in the following areas:

(1)   Ability to consider problems from different perspectives

(2)   Ability to identify and criticize the misuse of statistics

(3)   Ability to analyse quantitative evidence in a critical manner

 

Skills Outcomes

Generic Competencies

Graduates will have possessed the following generic skills:

(1)   Effective presentation skills to communicate findings/conclusions

(2)   The ability to work in teams

(3)   Competence in using information technology

 

Professional Skills

Graduates will have acquired a good mastery of professional skills, including the following:

(1)   Ability to formulate statistical problems

(2)   Ability to analyse data and use statistics accurately

(3)   Ability to communicate and convey the results of the analysis to anyone

 

Attitude/Value Outcomes

Graduates will have acquired a positive attitude towards statistical issues, including the following:

(1)   Awareness of the importance of statistics in daily life

(2)   Appreciation of different aspects of knowledge in the Information Age

(3)   Avoidance of the abuse of statistics

(4)   Appreciation of statistical theory

 

2.            Minor Programme:

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to Statistics such as sense of statistical consciousness and competence in information technology, acquired knowledge of data analysis and statistical inference, and to pursue a related career.
