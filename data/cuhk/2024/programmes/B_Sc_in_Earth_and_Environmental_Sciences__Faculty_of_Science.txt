Study Scheme
 

	

Earth and Environmental Sciences

Applicable to students admitted in 2024-25




 

 

There are three streams of specialization: 1) Atmospheric Science, 2) Geology and Geophysics, and 3) Environmental Science and Technology.  The stream selected by the student will be printed on the transcript.  Students admitted to the Earth and Environmental Sciences Major Programme through the broad-based admission scheme may apply at their final year of attendance to specialize in any of the streams and select the relevant courses as prescribed below.

Explanatory Notes:

	

 




1.

	

BCHE, BIOL, CHEM, CSCI, EEEN, EESC, ELEG, ENGG, ENSC, ESSC, GPAD, GRMD, IERG, LAWS, LSCI, MAEG, MATH, MBTE, PHPC, PHYS, RMSC, STAT, URSP courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

Students are strongly encouraged to take one more course in the remaining group to fulfill part of the Electives requirement in 3(d) in order to broaden their exposure to all areas of science.

[c]

	

Students may select research-oriented course(s), as approved by the Major Programme, to substitute up to 4 units for fulfillment of Research Courses requirement.




[d]

	

Students must complete any exchange/research/internship programme(s) offered by the University, Colleges, the Faculty of Science or Major Programme, as approved by the Major Programme, to fulfill the Experiential Learning requirement.  Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in the exchange/research/internship programme(s).

Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.        Major Programme:

 




K – A solid theoretical foundation in the Earth and Environmental Sciences not only as defined disciplines but also their inter-dependent as well as synergistic nature for developing a broad perspective of the interaction between humankind and the natural world.

 




S – Competency in generic (literacy, numeracy, communication) and problem-solving (team work, critical thinking, technical writing) as well as disciplinary skills, with particular emphasis on quantitative and analytical approaches in the Earth and Environmental Sciences that are relevant to the contemporary job market.

 




V – An inquisitive mind equipped with the necessary knowledge, skills and perspective for tackling the environmental challenges to long-term sustainability of human society at both the local and global levels.

 




2.        Minor Programme




 




Upon completion of this Minor Programme, students will have acquired:




 




•     a theoretical foundation in Earth and Environmental Sciences not only as defined discipline but also their inter-dependent as well as synergistic nature for developing a broad perspective of the interaction between humankind and the natural world. (K)

 




•     generic competency (literacy, numeracy, communication) and problem-solving (team work, critical thinking, technical writing) as well as discipline-specific skills, with particular emphasis on quantitative and analytical approaches in Earth and Environmental Sciences that are relevant to the contemporary job market. (S)

 




•     an inquisitive mind equipped with the necessary knowledge, skills and perspective for tackling environmental challenges to long-term sustainability of human society at both the local and global levels. (V)

 




 




K = Knowledge outcomes

S = Skills outcomes

V = Values and attitude outcomes
