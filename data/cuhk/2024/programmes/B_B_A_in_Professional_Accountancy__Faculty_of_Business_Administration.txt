Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

1.          Major Programme:

Upon completion of the Programme, students will:

(1)   have effective business communication skills needed by accounting professionals (S);

(2)   be able to solve business problems by analyzing, interpreting accounting, financial and other business information (K/S);

(3)   have the ability to integrate business knowledge and solve accounting problems in a business environment (K/S);

(4)   understand and identify business issues in a global environment (K/S);

(5)   be aware of the ethical issues in relation to accounting practices (V).

K = Knowledge outcomes  S = Skills outcomes  V = Values and attitudes outcomes
