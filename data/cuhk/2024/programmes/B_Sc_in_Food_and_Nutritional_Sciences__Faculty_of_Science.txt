Students admitted to the Food and Nutritional Sciences through the broad-based admission scheme may apply to specialize in the Nutritionist Stream by the end of second term in their second year of attendance. Students applying to specialize in this stream must have obtained (1) a GPA of 3.3 or above; (2) an overall result of at least 6.5 in IELTS with no less than 6.0 in each component; (3) at least a C in FNSC2005; and (4) recommendation from the selection panel. The minimum unit requirement for the Nutritionist Stream is 66.

FNSC courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

Study Option: Berkeley Biosciences Study Abroad Programme

Students who undergo one term of studies at the University of California, Berkeley, USA will take courses offered by the Berkeley Biosciences Study Abroad Programme. Units obtained from the Programme will be recognized as up to 12 units of Major Programme requirement. This option will be recorded on the transcript.

Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

Major Programme:




Upon completing the programme, students will have




 

	

 




Knowledge outcomes




(1)

	

a scientific foundation in food and nutritional sciences adequate to begin working effectively in the field.




(2)

	

an understanding of fundamental concepts and up-to-date issues in food and nutritional sciences.




(3)

	

an understanding of how to apply the knowledge in the food and nutritional sciences to practical situations in the food and nutritional sciences.




 

	

 




Skills outcomes




(1)

	

the ability to access, retrieve and critically evaluate relevant food science/nutritional science information.




(2)

	

the ability to make judgments about alternative perspectives and consider different points of view.




(3)

	

the ability to explore new things in a multi-disciplinary way.




(4)

	

the ability to engage in self-directed learning.




(5)

	

the ability to use knowledge to solve problems in food and nutritional sciences.




(6)

	

the ability to communicate effectively with a wide range of people in speaking and in writing.




(7)

	

the ability to work as an effective team or group member.
