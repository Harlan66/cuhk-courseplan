2.

	

Biology and Chemistry Double Major Programme:

 




Upon completion of the programme, students will have acquired the abilities as follows:




Knowledge outcomes:

To train our students to have fundamental and advanced knowledge of modern biological sciences and the ability of applying the acquired knowledge in exploring chemistry-related problems. Specifically, they include:




(1)

	

Basic knowledge in biology, including diversity and functioning of various living organisms




(2)

	

In‐depth understanding in at least one major area of biology, such as physiology, ecology and evolution, and human biology




(3)

	

Chemistry principles, concepts, and applications of inorganic chemistry, organic chemistry, physical chemistry, and analytical chemistry




(4)

	

Mathematical techniques required in chemical science




 

	

 




Skills outcomes:

With our team of teaching staff coming from different professional backgrounds and with teaching styles, students will be exposed to various learning activities and challenges throughout their 4 years of study, during which they can develop professional skills and generic attributes including active learning, self-learning, effective communication, critical thinking, team playing, as well as analytical and problem-solving skills. Specifically, they include:




(1)

	

Basic laboratory/field techniques in at least one major line of modern biological research, including molecular biology, physiology and field biology




(2)

	

Ability of recognizing key factors and applying appropriate assumption to formulate solutions for chemistry problems




(3)

	

Basic mathematical skills for handling experimental data and quantitative arguments in physical chemistry




(4)

	

Ability of determining molecular structure based on spectroscopic measurements




(5)

	

Ability to implement safety measures in conducting chemical laboratory works




(6)

	

Ability to operate instruments in chemistry laboratories




(7)

	

Ability in carrying out prescribed procedures using suited experimental skills and instrumentation




(8)

	

Ability to conduct literature reviews/experimental works and to report scientific findings in both oral and written formats




(9)

	

Competence in using commercial computer software for graphical depiction of molecular systems




(10)

	

Problem solving skills including ability to identify key issues and formulate feasible solutions of problems based on logical analysis and critical thinking




(11)

	

Ability to sort out complex observations to construct logical proposition




(12)

	

Ability of formulating logical sequence in arguments




(13)

	

Critical evaluation of issues based on evidence and scientific logic




(14)

	

Ability of analysing issues quantitatively with the sense of level of uncertainty and reliability




(15)

	

Ability to acquire new knowledge by self-study and to conduct independent work with logical planning




(16)

	

Interpersonal skills including the ability to work effectively in a group




(17)

	

Information retrieval skills through library and internet




(18)

	

Time management skills




 

	

 




Values and attitude outcomes:

V = We also concern about the well-being of our students. Apart from the long-established academic advisory system, there are ample chances for teachers and students to meet and exchange ideas formally and informally. These activities, organised by the programme, Biology Society, Chemistry Society or Alumni Association of the Department of Biology, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUBIOAA), include tea parties, dinners, BBQ, and career talks. From these, we believe our students are primed with positive values, and will be open and respectful to different views or ideas, so as to act as positive contributors in our society, no matter what fields and positions they are in. Our students also have the ability to appreciate the nature, and diversity of life and thus, would contributing to preserving our mother Earth. Specifically, they include:




(1)

	

Appreciation of biological knowledge and the beauty of chemistry and their importance as central science




(2)

	

Sense of biological conservation and other environmental issues




(3)

	

Awareness on the latest developments and advancements in biology




(4)

	

Awareness of impacts of chemistry in environmental, social, economic, industrial, and technological aspects




(5)

	

Awareness of chemical safety




(6)

	

Curiosity and interest in scientific exploration




(7)

	

Confidence in ability of solving problems




(8)

	

Motivation and ability for life-long learning




(9)

	

Sense of taking responsibility in study and work environments




 

	

 




3.

	

Minor Programme:

 




Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have a basic understanding of fundamental issues related to modern biology and acquired basic knowledge of cell biology, genetics and ecology to allow further study in subjects such as biotechnology and environmental sciences or to pursue a related career.
