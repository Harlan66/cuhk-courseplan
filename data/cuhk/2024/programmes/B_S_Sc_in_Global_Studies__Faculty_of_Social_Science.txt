Study Scheme


Learning Outcomes
 

	

 

1.          Major Programme:

 

Knowledge

Upon completion of this Programme, students will have gained:

•  a basic understanding of the economic, cultural, political, and environmental dimensions of globalization;

•  a strong appreciation of the challenges of globalization and the opportunities it presents for the growth and development of countries;

•  a solid grasp of the issues related to governance and conflicts in global contexts, geopolitics and the world in the media;

•  a working knowledge of social science research methods, including qualitative research, surveys, mathematical modeling, computer simulation, and data mining; and

•  specialized knowledge in at least two of the following five concentration areas: Global Media, Cultures and Societies, World Politics, Sustainability and Environment, and Global Economy.

 

Skills

Students of this Programme will have acquired skills in:

•  analytical and creative thinking through its emphasis on interdisciplinary and multicultural studies, and analyzing the complexity of global phenomena from multilateral perspectives;

•  multilingual and multicultural settings through studies in a foreign language and resident study overseas;

•  cross-cultural interactions and managing culturally diverse project teams through mandatory internships in multinational organizations; and

•  interdisciplinary and collaborative research through group research projects supervised by researchers from a wide range of disciplines.

 

Values and Attitudes

Students will have acquired the following values/attitudes:

•  global consciousness and awareness of cultural differences;

•  appreciation of the unique strengths of certain foreign cultures; and

•  the mentality to think globally and act locally.




 




2.     Minor Programme:

 

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
